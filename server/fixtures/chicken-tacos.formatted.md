## Ingredients

### Assembly

- 8 small flour tortillas
- Half a red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon granulated sugar
- Avocado
- 1 cup thinly\-sliced or shaved red cabbage
- Handful roughly\-chopped cilantro
- Hot sauce of your choice

### Chicken

- 1 teaspoon kosher salt \(Diamond brand; use less of others\)
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili or chipotle powder
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked or regular paprika
- Many grinds of black pepper
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 to 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large garlic clove, minced
- 2/3 cup chicken broth

## Method

### Preparation

1. Wrap the tortillas in foil, or place them in a tortilla warmer or similar dish, and place in a 350\-degree oven\.
2. Combine the red onion, red wine vinegar, water, kosher salt, and granulated sugar\. Set aside to begin pickling\. Optionally, microwave the bowl for 10 to 15 seconds, just until the liquid gets hot, then leave aside to cool while preparing the remaining ingredients\.

### Chicken

3. Combine the salt, cumin, chili powder, paprika, and pepper in a small bowl\.
4. Spread the chicken thighs out on a tray and sprinkle each side of the cutlets lightly with the seasoning\.
5. Heat a large skillet over medium\-high heat until very hot, then add the olive oil\. Once the oil is hot, arrange the chicken in one layer and cook until browned underneath, about 4 to 5 minutes\. Flip the chicken and cook until browned on the second side; it does not need to be cooked through at this stage\. Transfer the chicken briefly to a plate\.
6. In the empty skillet, add the garlic and cook for a minute, until it turns golden\. Add the broth and use it to scrape any stuck bits off the pan\. Let it come to a simmer\. Return the chicken and any collected juices to the pan, reduce the heat to medium, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the broth has reduced to a thin puddle\.
7. Turn off the heat\. Shred the chicken in the pan with two forks, or use the edge of a spatula to cut it into bite\-sized pieces\. Stir the chicken around in any liquid left\.

### Assembly

8. Pile each warm tortilla with shredded chicken, any juices left in the pan, and all of the toppings\.

## Notes

- Time: 30 minutes, with assembly\.
