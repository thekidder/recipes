Cucumber Salad with Sesame and Rice Vinegar
Servings: 2 to 4, theoretically Time: 15 minutes, plus resting time Source: Smitten Kitchen
1 pound (455 grams) Persian-style cucumbers, trimmed, in 1/2-inch slices
1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt (Diamond brand, use half of others)
3 tablespoons (45 ml) unseasoned rice vinegar
1 tablespoon (15 ml) toasted sesame oil
1 tablespoon (15 ml) soy sauce
Ground black or white pepper
Chili oil or chili crisp to taste, or red pepper flakes
Toasted sesame seeds

Combine cucumbers and salt in a bowl. Set aside for as little as 10 to 15 minutes, or up to an hour or two. Transfer to the fridge to keep it cold and refreshing.
Drain any liquid from the cucumber bowl, then add rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, pepper, and additional salt, if needed. Stir to combine. Finish with chili oil and toasted sesame seeds.

Do ahead: Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 to 3 days, and they’re amazing, becoming gently pickled.
