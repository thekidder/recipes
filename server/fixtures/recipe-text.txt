Lemon chickpea salad
Serves 4. Prep: 15 minutes.

Ingredients:
2 cans chickpeas, drained
1 cucumber, diced
3 tbsp olive oil
2 tbsp lemon juice
1/2 tsp salt

Method:
1. Rinse and drain the chickpeas well.
2. Combine the chickpeas and cucumber in a bowl.
3. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice and salt together.
4. Toss the salad with the dressing. Serve at room temperature.
